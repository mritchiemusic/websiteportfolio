Mike Ritchie

Immersive Audio Engineer and Composer

For over 15 years I've been creating immersive soundscapes using the latest advancements in spatial audio. As a sonic storyteller, I strive to create meaningful artistic experiences while keeping emotional intention at the forefront.

I've worked on interactive experiences, large scale exhibitions, and industry leading XR projects with Felix and Paul Studios. I've also composed five albums of ambient music as well as the soundtrack to the award winning documentary Forest Man.

I specialize in tasteful and evocative audio with an emphasis on realism and immersion, using my love of music and film to tell stories through sound. Feel free to say hello!
