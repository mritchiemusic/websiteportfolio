Mike Ritchie

Immersive Audio Engineer and Composer

I've been working as an immersive audio engineer for over 17 years, in pursuit of soundscapes that transport the listener and use spatial mediums to evoke powerful emotions.

I’ve contributed to Emmy Award winning work with Felix and Paul Studios, including virtual reality experiences with NASA, Eminem, and the NBA.

I’ve also worked on interactive experiences, large scale exhibitions, and composed six albums of ambient music. If it involves sound, I’ve probably done it. Feel free to say hello, I’m always happy to talk audio.
