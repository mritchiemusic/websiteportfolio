Mike Ritchie

Immersive Audio Engineer and Composer

For over a decade I've been creating immersive soundscapes using the latest advancements in spatial audio. I am a sonic storyteller, always keeping the emotional intent at the forefront of projects to create meaningful artistic experiences.

I've worked on interactive experiences, large scale exhibitions, and industry leading XR projects with Felix and Pauls Studios. I've also composed six albums of ambient music and produced other artists.

I specialize in tasteful and evocative audio, with an emphasis on realism and immersion, using my love of music and film to tell stories through sound. Feel free to say hello!
