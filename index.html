Mike Ritchie

Immersive Audio Engineer and Composer

For over 17 years I've been pursuing soundscapes that evoke powerful emotions through immersive audio.

I've contributed spatial audio mixing, sound design, foley, location recording, and interactive development with Emmy Award winning Felix and Paul Studios and other industry leading companies. Projects include interactive experiences, large scale exhibitions, and XR experiences. I've also composed six albums of ambient music.

If it involves sound, I've probably done it. Feel free to say hello!
